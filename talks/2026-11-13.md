---
title: The Geometry of Cochains on Sampled Vietoris-Rips Complexes
author: Darrick Lee 
date: 2026-11-13
category: talk
---
In manifold learning, we aim to approximate geometric properties of an unknown embedded manifold from a sampled point cloud by building a discrete analogue. For instance, we can approximate the Laplace Beltrami operator using the graph Laplacian of an appropriately weighted graph. In this talk, we describe a framework for approximating differential forms using simplicial complexes on the Vietoris Rips complex. We equip these cochains with inner products based defined using ambient distances, and discuss probabilistic convergence results of these discrete constructions to their continuum analogues. We will also consider their consequences for the discrete Hodge Laplacian. Based on joint work with Kelly Maggs.
