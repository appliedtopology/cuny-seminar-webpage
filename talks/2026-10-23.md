---
title: Topology-Preserving Scientific Data Compression
author: Bei Wang
date: 2026-10-23
category: talk
---

Scientific simulations and instruments generate data at volumes that
make lossy compression essential for practical storage and transfer.
Yet conventional compressors, even those with strict pointwise error
bounds, can alter the geometric and topological features that
scientists rely on for analysis, including isosurfaces, merge trees,
and Morse–Smale complexes. This talk presents general frameworks for
topology-preserving scientific data compression across scalar, vector,
and tensor fields. These approaches preserve a broad class of
geometric and topological features while achieving compression ratios
comparable to, and in some cases better than, specialized methods.
Together, they provide a flexible path toward more reliable compressed
scientific data and more trustworthy downstream analysis. This talk is
based on joint works with Nathaniel Gorski, Xin Liang, Hanqi Guo, and
Lin Yan.
