title: "Field notes: AI in the lab"
author: Aryan Thakur 
date: 2026-09-18
category: talk

How are researchers actually using AI? Drawing on conversations with heavy AI adopters in labs at Harvard, Stanford, and UChicago, this talk will highlight strategies that are working. We’ll discuss real use cases from heavy adopters, like agents that handle analysis "no grad student wants to do" and nightly literature bots. We’ll also cover the mental models that super-users are using to think about adapting to rapid model improvement. Aryan Thakur is part of the founding team at Tuva, a startup building tools to help researchers get the most out of AI.
