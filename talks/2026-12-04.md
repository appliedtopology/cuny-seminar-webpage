---
title: Regulatory-network Informed Homeostatic Transport Plans in scRNA seq
author: Kelly Maggs
date: 2026-12-04
category: talk
---

A common problem in single cell sequencing is to reverse engineer dynamics from single cell RNA seq data. In this work, we focus on the case that biological systems are in homeostasis — that is, they are undergoing measure preserving, divergence free dynamics. Under this assumption, we propose a method of incorporating known regulatory relations into the cost function of an optimal transport problem such that there is a canonical best solution given a parameter that balances the regulatory relations against the regularity of the dynamics. We demonstrate how our model works on both synthetic and real-world biological data. 

This is joint work with Heather Harrington, Asgar Jamneshan, Leyi Jiang, Martin Keller-Ressel and Maximilian Wiesmann.
